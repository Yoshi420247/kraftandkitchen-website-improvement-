Frequently Asked Questions

Is this single-sided or double-sided?

This bulk product is single-sided (silicone coating on one side only). The coated side is the release surface. The uncoated side can be printed on with laser printers.

Can I print on this paper?

Yes, you can print on the uncoated (non-silicone) side with laser printers. We recommend testing a small batch before production runs. Inkjet printing is not recommended as ink won't properly absorb on either side.

Is this stronger than parchment paper?

Yes. At 40 lb basis weight with silicone coating, this paper is more durable and provides more consistent release than standard parchment paper. It's designed for repeated handling in production environments.

What's the difference between letter and legal size?

Letter size (8.5" x 11") is standard printer paper size and fits most binders and storage systems. Legal size (8.5" x 14") gives you 3 extra inches of length, useful for larger projects or when you need to trim custom sizes.

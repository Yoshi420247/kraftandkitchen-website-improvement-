3 mil vs 4 mil

  • 3 mil: Standard weight, more flexible, slightly better heat transfer. Good for most applications.
  • 4 mil: Heavy duty, stiffer, longer-lasting under heavy production use. Choose for high-volume shops.

Cutting Tips

  • Use sharp scissors or a rotary cutter with cutting mat
  • Mark measurements with painter's tape (won't leave residue)
  • Cut slightly oversize - you can always trim down
  • Store cut sheets flat immediately to prevent curling

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy rolls or pre-cut sheets?

For most home crafters and small businesses, pre-cut sheets are more convenient and cost-effective when you factor in your time. Rolls make sense when you need custom sizes, very high volume, or are operating multiple presses with different platen dimensions.

Is this the same material as the pre-cut sheets?

Yes. Our rolls and pre-cut sheets use identical PTFE-coated fiberglass material. The only difference is the format.

How should I store the roll?

Store rolls in a cool, dry place. Keep the roll in its original packaging or wrap loosely to prevent dust accumulation. Cut sheets should be stored flat - rolling cut sheets can cause permanent curl.

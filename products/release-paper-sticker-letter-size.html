The Right Paper for Sticker Storage

Our double-sided silicone release paper is designed for crafters who need reliable, reusable sheets for organizing stickers, creating sticker books, and backing decals.

The silicone coating on both sides means you can use every inch of the sheet. Stickers peel away cleanly without losing their adhesive—even after months of storage.

Why Crafters Choose This Paper

  • Sticker book pages: Letter size fits standard 3-ring binders. Build your sticker collection without special supplies.
  • Temporary sticker storage: Keep stickers organized between projects. They stay sticky and peel-ready.
  • Decal backing sheets: Smooth release surface for vinyl decals and transfer applications.
  • Craft organization: Store die cuts, washi tape samples, and small adhesive items.

Silicone Release Paper vs. Parchment Paper

Parchment paper can work in a pinch, but silicone release paper is specifically engineered for adhesive applications. The difference:

  • Silicone coating provides consistent release across the entire surface
  • Won't transfer fibers or residue to your stickers
  • More durable for repeated stick-and-peel cycles
  • Results may vary by sticker type—always test with your specific materials

How to Use

Sticker Storage & Organization

How to Use

Place stickers directly onto the release paper. They'll stay in place but peel off cleanly when you're ready to use them.

Why It Works

The silicone coating creates a low-tack surface. Adhesive sticks enough to stay put, but releases without leaving residue or losing stickiness.

Pro Tips

  • Store sheets flat in a binder or folder—avoid rolling
  • Keep away from direct sunlight to preserve sticker adhesive
  • Label your sheets with washi tape for easy organization

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't store heavy items on top of sticker sheets (can flatten adhesive)
  • Avoid humid storage areas
Creating Sticker Books

How to Use

Punch holes in sheets using a standard 3-hole punch. Insert into any letter-size binder. Add stickers to both sides of each page.

Why It Works

Double-sided coating means both faces of each page are usable. Standard letter size means no special binders or supplies needed.

Pro Tips

  • Use sheet protectors between pages if storing valuable stickers
  • Create dividers with cardstock to organize by theme or type
  • Start with 25-50 sheets—you can always add more

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't overfill binders—pages need room to lay flat
  • Avoid cheap binder rings that can scratch sheets
Decal & Transfer Backing

How to Use

Apply vinyl decals or transfers to the release paper for storage or as a temporary carrier. Peel and apply when ready.

Why It Works

Silicone provides consistent release across the entire sheet surface, ensuring clean transfer without adhesive damage.

Pro Tips

  • Burnish (rub) decals firmly onto release paper for better temporary adhesion
  • Store flat between uses
  • Works well for weeded HTV designs waiting to be pressed

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't leave decals on release paper for extended periods (6+ months)—some adhesives can change
  • Test with your specific vinyl brand first
Heat Press Barrier Sheet

How to Use

Place between your design and heat press platen as a protective barrier. Can be used for low-temperature applications.

Why It Works

Silicone coating provides non-stick protection. However, for high-temperature heat press work, we recommend PTFE cover sheets instead.

Temperature Limits

Silicone release paper is suitable for lower-temperature applications. For temperatures above 300°F, use our PTFE Cover Sheets which are rated to 500°F.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't use for high-temperature sublimation (use PTFE instead)
  • Replace if paper shows any signs of heat damage

Does It Replace Parchment Paper?

For sticker and adhesive applications, silicone release paper outperforms parchment:

  • More consistent release: Silicone coating is specifically engineered for adhesive contact
  • More durable: Handles repeated stick-peel cycles better than parchment
  • No fiber transfer: Won't leave paper fibers on sticky surfaces

That said, results can vary based on the specific stickers or adhesives you're using. We always recommend testing with your materials before committing to a large batch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it double-sided?

Yes. Both sides have silicone coating, so stickers release cleanly from either surface. This doubles the usable space on each sheet.

Will my stickers lose their stickiness?

No. Silicone release paper is designed to release adhesives without removing them. Your stickers should retain their full stickiness when removed. For best results, avoid storing stickers for more than 6-12 months, as some adhesives can naturally degrade over time regardless of storage method.

Can I print on this paper?

Laser printers work with this paper, though we recommend testing first. Inkjet printers are not recommended—the silicone coating prevents proper ink absorption and can cause smearing.

What pack size should I start with?

For personal sticker books: 25-50 sheets is plenty to start. For small business or heavy crafting: 100 sheets offers the best per-sheet value without over-committing. The 250-sheet pack is ideal for sticker makers and small businesses.

Is this the same as wax paper?

No. Wax paper has a wax coating that can transfer to adhesives and isn't designed for repeated use. Silicone release paper has a more durable, non-transferring coating specifically made for adhesive applications.

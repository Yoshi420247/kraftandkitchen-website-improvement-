# Photography Shot List and Guidelines

Photography is critical for craft products. Craft buyers make decisions based on seeing products in use, not just product specs. This guide covers the shots you need.

---

## General Photography Principles

### Show Scale and Context
- Include hands, common objects, or measurements to show size
- Craft shoppers need to understand dimensions instantly

### Show Use Cases
- Products in action are more compelling than product-only shots
- Multiple use case shots help different shopper segments

### Maintain Consistency
- Same lighting setup for all products
- Same background style within categories
- Consistent editing/color treatment

### Quality Standards
- Minimum 2000x2000 pixels for Shopify zoom
- Clean, well-lit images
- Sharp focus on product
- Neutral or contextual backgrounds

## PTFE Cover Sheets - Required Shots

### Shot 1: Sheet on Heat Press Platen (Hero Shot)
**Purpose:** Shows scale and primary use case
**Setup:**
- Heat press with platen exposed
- PTFE sheet laid flat on platen
- Sheet should extend slightly beyond edges
- Press can be open at angle for visibility

**Notes:**
- This is your main product image
- Should clearly show sheet size relative to press

### Shot 2: Sheet Covering a Design
**Purpose:** Shows product in action
**Setup:**
- PTFE sheet over a shirt/substrate with design visible underneath
- Shows the "barrier" positioning
- Can show partial press closed or setup before pressing

**Notes:**
- Design should be colorful/visible through translucent sheet
- Demonstrates the primary use case

### Shot 3: Close-Up Texture Shot
**Purpose:** Shows material quality and weave
**Setup:**
- Macro or close-up shot of sheet surface
- Shows fiberglass weave texture (if applicable)
- Neutral background

**Notes:**
- Helps shoppers understand what they're getting
- Distinguishes quality PTFE from cheap alternatives

### Shot 4: Cleaning/Wipe Demo
**Purpose:** Shows reusability and care
**Setup:**
- Hand wiping sheet with cloth
- Can show "before/after" of residue removal
- Demonstrates easy maintenance

**Notes:**
- Reinforces the reusable value proposition
- Shows how to care for the product

### Shot 5: Comparison Shot (Optional)
**Purpose:** Shows difference vs. alternatives
**Setup:**
- Side-by-side: PTFE sheet vs. parchment paper result
- Or: clean platen with cover vs. dirty platen without
- Must be honest and repeatable

**Notes:**
- Only if you can consistently reproduce the comparison
- Should not be misleading

### Shot 6: Flat Storage Shot
**Purpose:** Shows proper storage and care
**Setup:**
- Sheets stored flat (not rolled or folded)
- Can show hanging on hook or lying flat
- Include "do not crease" visual messaging

**Notes:**
- Educational shot that prevents customer issues
- Can include text overlay: "Store flat - do not fold"

### Shot 7: Pack/Quantity Shot
**Purpose:** Shows what customer receives
**Setup:**
- Neat stack of sheets (for multi-packs)
- Or single sheet laid out with dimensions visible
- Include measurement or ruler for scale

## Silicone Release Paper - Required Shots

### Shot 1: Sticker Peel Shot (Hero for Sticker Use)
**Purpose:** Shows clean release action
**Setup:**
- Sticker being peeled from release paper
- Shows sticker maintaining adhesive
- Clean, bright background

**Notes:**
- This is the money shot for sticker storage customers
- Should look satisfying and clean

### Shot 2: Sticker Book Page Setup
**Purpose:** Shows organizational use case
**Setup:**
- Release paper with multiple stickers organized
- In a binder or showing hole-punched edges
- Shows both sides usable (if double-sided)

**Notes:**
- Appeals to sticker book/collector audience
- Shows practical storage application

### Shot 3: Diamond Painting Use (for 4x4 squares)
**Purpose:** Shows diamond painting use case
**Setup:**
- Diamond painting canvas with release paper squares positioned
- Shows squares covering sections of exposed adhesive
- Canvas partially completed for context

**Notes:**
- Critical for diamond painting collection
- Should show "sectioned" working method

### Shot 4: Close-Up Coating Sheen
**Purpose:** Shows silicone coating quality
**Setup:**
- Macro shot showing smooth, slightly shiny surface
- Neutral background
- Shows coating consistency

### Shot 5: Pack Layout Shot
**Purpose:** Shows quantity and size
**Setup:**
- Stack of sheets with top sheet visible
- Include count indicator or packaging
- Show size reference (ruler or common object)

**Notes:**
- Helps customer understand pack size
- Different shot needed for each pack variant

### Shot 6: Multiple Use Cases (Lifestyle Grid)
**Purpose:** Shows versatility
**Setup:**
- Grid of 4 smaller images:
  - Sticker storage
  - Decal backing
  - Diamond painting
  - Heat press use (if applicable)

**Notes:**
- Can be created from other shots
- Good for collection page headers

## Diamond Painting Squares - Required Shots

### Shot 1: Squares on Canvas (Hero Shot)
**Purpose:** Shows product in actual use
**Setup:**
- Diamond painting canvas in progress
- 4x4 squares covering unexposed sections
- Some diamonds placed for context
- Clean, well-lit workspace

**Notes:**
- This is the most important shot for this product
- Shows exactly how the product is used

### Shot 2: Square Size Reference
**Purpose:** Shows dimensions
**Setup:**
- Single square with ruler or hand for scale
- "4 x 4" dimensions visible or implied
- Clean background

### Shot 3: Pack Quantity Shot
**Purpose:** Shows what customer receives
**Setup:**
- Neat stack or spread of squares
- Show approximate quantity (100 or 200 pack)
- Can include packaging

### Shot 4: Section Coverage Demo
**Purpose:** Educational shot
**Setup:**
- Canvas with one section exposed (working area)
- Adjacent sections covered with squares
- Shows the "work-and-protect" method

### Shot 5: Before/After Concept (Optional)
**Purpose:** Shows the problem/solution
**Setup:**
- Split image: dusty exposed adhesive vs. clean protected section
- Demonstrates why release paper matters

**Notes:**
- Only if you can create this honestly
- Should be subtle, not dramatic

## Product Comparison Shots (If Used)

If you include comparison imagery:

### Guidelines
- **Be honest:** Only show real, repeatable differences
- **Be fair:** Use comparable conditions for both items
- **Be clear:** Label what's being compared
- **Avoid claims:** Let the image speak without superlatives

### Acceptable Comparisons
- PTFE sheet vs. uncovered (showing platen protection benefit)
- Release paper vs. original plastic cover (showing flexibility benefit)
- Organized stickers on release paper vs. loose stickers

### Not Recommended
- Exaggerated failure scenarios
- Unrealistic conditions
- Claims that can't be verified

## Background and Styling Guidelines

### For Product Shots (Spec/Detail)
- Clean white or light neutral background
- Consistent lighting
- Product as hero

### For Use Case Shots (Lifestyle)
- Realistic craft workspace
- Clean but authentic setting
- Include hands when showing use
- Context objects (press, canvas, binder) should look real

### For Hero Shots
- Bright, clean, professional
- Can include slight gradient or texture
- Product should "pop" from background

## Image File Naming Convention

Use consistent naming for easy organization:

```
[product]-[shot-type]-[variant].jpg

Examples:
ptfe-cover-sheet-hero-16x20.jpg
ptfe-cover-sheet-on-press-16x20.jpg
ptfe-cover-sheet-texture-closeup.jpg
release-paper-sticker-peel-letter.jpg
release-paper-sticker-book-letter.jpg
diamond-painting-squares-on-canvas-4x4.jpg
diamond-painting-squares-pack-200.jpg
```

## Image Count Summary

### Per PTFE Cover Sheet Product
- 6-7 images minimum
- Include hero, use case, texture, care, pack shots

### Per Release Paper Product
- 5-6 images minimum
- Include hero, peel shot, organizational use, pack shot

### Per Diamond Painting Squares Product
- 5 images minimum
- Include on-canvas hero, size reference, pack quantity

### Total Photo Needs (Estimated)
- PTFE Cover Sheets: 7 shots × variants = 14-21 images
- Release Paper Letter: 6 shots × variants = 12-18 images
- Diamond Painting Squares: 5 shots × variants = 10-15 images
- Bulk/Roll Products: 4-5 shots each

**Total: 50-75 product images** for a complete refresh

---

## Prioritization

If shooting in phases, prioritize:

### Phase 1 (Critical)
1. PTFE cover sheet hero (on press)
2. Release paper sticker peel (for sticker audience)
3. Diamond painting squares on canvas
4. Pack/quantity shots for all products

### Phase 2 (High Value)
5. Use case shots (covering design, sticker book, etc.)
6. Size/scale reference shots
7. Texture close-ups

### Phase 3 (Complete Set)
8. Care/storage educational shots
9. Comparison shots (if appropriate)
10. Lifestyle grid images

---

## Notes for Photographer

- **Hands:** Include hands in use case shots - makes images relatable
- **Lighting:** Soft, even lighting prevents harsh shadows on shiny surfaces
- **Focus:** Sharp focus on product; slight blur on background OK for lifestyle
- **Props:** Use real craft supplies (heat press, stickers, diamonds) not fake/staged items
- **Editing:** Keep colors accurate; don't over-saturate; maintain consistency

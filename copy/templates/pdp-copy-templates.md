# Product Page Copy Templates

Repeatable templates for generating consistent, high-converting product page copy.

---

## Voice Rules

1. **Use plain craft language first**, then material in parentheses
   - ✅ "Heat press cover sheet (PTFE)"
   - ❌ "PTFE sheet for heat transfer applications"

2. **Be specific**, avoid hype words
   - ✅ "Rated to 500°F"
   - ❌ "The best heat-resistant material on the market"

3. **If something varies by setup, say "test first"**
   - ✅ "May add slight semi-gloss finish; test with your materials"
   - ❌ "Creates a beautiful glossy finish every time"

4. **Focus on the job, not the product**
   - ✅ "Protects your platen from residue"
   - ❌ "High-quality PTFE construction"

## Template A: PTFE Cover Sheets

### Title Format
```
PTFE Heat Press Cover Sheet {Size}
```
Examples:
- PTFE Heat Press Cover Sheet 16" x 20"
- PTFE Heat Press Cover Sheet 18" x 20"

### One-Liner (max 120 chars)
```
A reusable nonstick sheet that protects your heat press platen from vinyl residue and ink transfer.
```

### 3 Bullet Benefits
```
• Best for: HTV, sublimation, DTF, screen print curing
• Size: {size} | Thickness: {thickness} mil | Pack: {count}
• Care: Wipe clean, store flat, replace if creased
```

### How to Use (3 Steps)
```
1. Place your design on the garment
2. Cover with PTFE sheet
3. Press at your normal settings (may need 2-3 extra seconds; test first)
```

### Specs Table Entries
```
| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Size | {size_inches} ({size_cm}) |
| Thickness | {thickness_mil} mil ({thickness_mm} mm) |
| Material | PTFE-coated fiberglass |
| Max Temperature | {temp_max_f}°F ({temp_max_c}°C) |
| Color | Beige/tan |
| Finish | Smooth (may impart semi-gloss) |
| Reusable | Yes - hundreds of uses |
| Storage | Flat - do not fold |
```

### FAQs (8)
```
Q: Will this change my press settings?
A: The cover sheet adds a thin barrier. You may need 2-3 extra seconds of press time. Always test with your specific materials.

Q: Is this the same as a Teflon sheet?
A: Yes. PTFE is the material used in Teflon (a DuPont brand name). "PTFE sheet," "Teflon sheet," and "cover sheet" all refer to this type of product.

Q: How many times can I reuse it?
A: Hundreds of times with proper care. Replace when creased, torn, or the surface becomes rough.

Q: What size should I get?
A: Match your heat press size. 16x20 fits most standard presses. 18x20 works for larger commercial presses.

Q: Will it affect the finish on my designs?
A: PTFE can add a slight semi-gloss finish to some materials. Test first. For matte finishes, some users place kraft paper between the design and PTFE sheet.

Q: How do I clean it?
A: Wipe with a damp cloth while still warm. For stubborn residue, use a plastic scraper. Never use metal tools.

Q: Can I use this for sublimation?
A: Yes. It's essential for sublimation to prevent ink from transferring to your platen.

Q: What's the difference between 3 mil and 4 mil?
A: 3 mil is more flexible, slightly better heat transfer. 4 mil is stiffer and more durable for heavy production. Start with 3 mil for most uses.
```

## Template B: Silicone Release Paper (Stickers/Transfers)

### Title Format
```
Silicone Release Paper Sheets {Size} ({Use Case})
```
Examples:
- Silicone Release Paper Sheets 8.5" x 11" (Sticker Book Pages)
- Silicone Release Paper Sheets 8.5" x 14" (Studio Pack)

### One-Liner (max 120 chars)
```
Double-sided nonstick paper for sticker storage, sticker books, and transfer backing. Stickers peel clean.
```

### 3 Bullet Benefits
```
• Best for: Sticker books, sticker storage, transfers, decal backing
• Size: {size} | Coating: {coating_sides}-sided | Pack: {count} sheets
• Features: 40lb weight, moisture resistant, laser printer safe (test first)
```

### How to Use (3 Steps) - Stickers
```
1. Place stickers on the release paper
2. Peel and re-place without losing adhesive
3. Store flat in a binder or folder
```

### How to Use (3 Steps) - Transfers
```
1. Apply transfer to release paper as backing
2. Cut or weed your design
3. Peel transfer cleanly when ready to apply
```

### Specs Table Entries
```
| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Size | {size_inches} ({size_cm}) |
| Coating | {coating_sides}-sided silicone |
| Basis Weight | 40 lb (60 gsm) |
| Color | White |
| Finish | Smooth, semi-gloss |
| Moisture Resistant | Yes |
| pH Balanced | Yes |
| Printer | Laser only (test on uncoated side) |
```

### FAQs (8)
```
Q: Is it double-sided?
A: {If double} Yes, both sides have silicone coating. Stickers peel clean from either face. {If single} This is single-sided. One side has silicone coating; the other is standard paper.

Q: Will my stickers lose their stickiness?
A: No. Silicone release paper is designed to release adhesives without removing them. Stickers retain their full adhesive.

Q: Can I print on this paper?
A: Laser printers work on the {uncoated/either} side. We recommend testing before production. Inkjet is not recommended.

Q: Does it fit standard binders?
A: {If 8.5x11} Yes, letter size fits standard 3-ring binders. Use a 3-hole punch to add pages. {If 8.5x14} Legal size is 3 inches longer than letter. Trim or use oversized binders.

Q: Can I use this for heat press?
A: Silicone paper works for lower-temperature applications. For high-heat pressing (300°F+), we recommend PTFE cover sheets instead.

Q: Is this the same as parchment paper?
A: Silicone release paper is engineered specifically for adhesives. It's more durable, has more consistent release, and won't transfer fibers like parchment can.

Q: What pack size should I start with?
A: 25-50 sheets for personal sticker books. 100 sheets for active crafters. 250+ for small businesses.

Q: How long will stickers stay fresh on the paper?
A: Stickers can stay for months. Some adhesives naturally degrade over 6-12 months regardless of storage. Keep away from heat and direct sunlight.
```

## Template C: Diamond Painting Release Paper Squares

### Title Format
```
Diamond Painting Release Paper Squares {Size} (Double-Sided)
```
Examples:
- Diamond Painting Release Paper Squares 4" x 4" (Double-Sided)
- Diamond Painting Release Paper Squares 4.5" x 4.5" (Double-Sided)

### One-Liner (max 120 chars)
```
Pre-cut squares that protect your diamond painting adhesive while you work section by section.
```

### 3 Bullet Benefits
```
• Best for: Diamond painting canvas protection
• Size: {size} pre-cut squares | Coating: Double-sided | Pack: {count} squares
• Use: Cover sections to keep adhesive clean from dust and debris
```

### How to Use (3 Steps)
```
1. Remove original plastic cover from one section
2. Place release paper squares over exposed adhesive you're not working on
3. Move squares as you complete sections; re-cover when you take a break
```

### Specs Table Entries
```
| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Size | {size} ({size_cm}) |
| Coating | Double-sided silicone |
| Basis Weight | 40 lb (60 gsm) |
| Color | White |
| Reusable | Yes - until creased or soiled |
| Works With | Poured glue canvases |
```

### FAQs (8)
```
Q: Does it work with poured glue canvases?
A: Yes. Silicone coating releases cleanly from poured glue adhesive without damaging the sticky surface.

Q: Is it double-sided?
A: Yes. Both sides have silicone coating, so you can use either side against the adhesive.

Q: How many squares do I need?
A: 8-12 squares can cover a typical 12x16 canvas while you work. 100-pack for 1-2 projects; 200-pack for multiple projects.

Q: Why 4x4 squares?
A: 4x4 is the standard size used by diamond painting suppliers. Large enough to cover sections, small enough to position precisely.

Q: Can I reuse them?
A: Yes. Reuse until creased, dirty, or the surface becomes rough. Store flat.

Q: Is this better than the plastic that came with my canvas?
A: The original plastic works until you cut it away. Release paper lets you re-cover any section at any time—including areas you've already exposed.

Q: Will the squares stick to my canvas?
A: They lay lightly on the adhesive but peel off cleanly. Don't press hard—a gentle placement is all you need.

Q: Can I cut larger sheets instead?
A: Yes, you can cut our letter-size release paper to any size. Pre-cut squares just save time.
```
